## Welcome to my blog

This is the place I share my thoughts and experiences on the various projects I embark on.

Crankshaft Technology is a name that came to me many years ago, and since a good friend of mine put so much time into creating the logo for me, it has stuck around every since. No, I don't do anything with cars or other machines with crankshafts, but it symbolises that many of my projects support the core functions of our everyday lives, making them better in subtle ways.
